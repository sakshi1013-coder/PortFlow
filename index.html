Platform Capabilities

Designed to address gate gridlock, yard reshuffles, and crane deadhead travel for container terminals handling over 1.4 million TEU annually.

Yard Allocation by Departure Order

Stacks outbound containers in reverse departure order so top containers leave first. Eliminates unproductive bottom-container entrapment and drops re-handling to the 1.25 target.

Crane Sequencing & Twin-Lift

Schedules dual-cycling and spreader trajectories across RTG and STS cranes, minimizing unladen spreader moves and boosting berth productivity past 30 moves/crane-hour.

Gate Slot Appointments

Replaces walk-in queuing with dynamic TAS booking windows and OCR optical lane clearance. Flattens the 71-minute queue down to the strict 30-minute operational threshold.

Planning / Execution Separation

Isolates complex solver engines from execution telemetry. Even if the optimization engine reboots, yard cranes continue uninterrupted from published local cache plans.

Historical Replay Testing

Validates scheduling algorithms against 3 full years of terminal execution logs (1.4M TEU/yr) to guarantee regression-free releases before publishing to live yard cranes.

Risk & Schedule Tracking

Continuous PERT/CPM analysis across Activities A–G. Delivers 85.9% probability of beating the 34-week deadline and calculates optimal crash costs against ₹12L/week penalties.

FOUR-STAGE WORKFLOW ENGINE

How It Works

From voyage manifest publication to real-time crane execution and telemetry verification.

01

Plan

Yard allocation algorithms assign blocks and slots based on container ETD, weight classes, and departure vessel sequence.

02

Publish

Published yard work orders and gate appointments are pushed to the resilient execution cache with cryptographic checksums.

03

Execute

Real-time crane onboard units and gate OCR terminals execute lift orders with zero round-trip dependency on planning engines.

04

Measure

Telemetry feeds continuous re-handle calculations and queue duration analytics back into the terminal KPI dashboard.
